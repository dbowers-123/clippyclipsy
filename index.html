ClippyClipsy

A personal content tool

ClippyClipsy is a personal tool operated by a single creator. It prepares short vertical gaming and livestream highlight videos with captions and publishes them to the operator's own social media accounts, including TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

The tool connects to TikTok only through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, and only to the TikTok account of the person who operates it. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from visitors to this website.
