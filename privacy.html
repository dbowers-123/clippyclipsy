Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

ClippyClipsy ("the Tool") is a personal tool operated by a single creator to publish short videos to the operator's own social media accounts. Contact: dbowers4155@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

When the operator connects their own TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit, the Tool receives an access token and basic account information (such as the account's display name and identifier) for that account. The Tool does not collect personal information from any other people. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and does not collect information from its visitors.

3. How the information is used

The access token is used only to upload and publish videos to the operator's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer. They are not sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party, other than being sent to TikTok to carry out the posting requests the operator initiates.

5. Retention and removal

Tokens are kept only while the connection is needed. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services (wording may vary), after which the stored token is deleted. You can also email the address above to ask for any data related to you to be deleted.

6. Children

The Tool is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
